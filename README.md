# Boston Area Stuff to Do

An interactive, locals-first resource for the Greater Boston area — free-to-moderate,
hidden gems over tourist traps. **It's a single self-contained web page** (`index.html`),
no build step and no dependencies.

## Use it

- **Open `index.html`** in any browser (double-click it, or host it).
- Pick a guide from the top menu: **Outdoors & free pickleball · Beaches · Day Trips ·
  Eat · Events & Markets · Indoors**.
- Inside a guide, use the **filter buttons** to show only what's **Free**, **$ Cheap**,
  **$$ Moderate**, **Weekday-friendly**, or **Car-free**.
- Works on phone and desktop, light and dark mode, and prints cleanly.

## Put it online (GitHub Pages)

Settings → Pages → Source: **Deploy from a branch** → Branch **main** / **/(root)** → Save.
After ~1–3 minutes it's live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/boston-local-guide/`.

## Notes

Compiled July 2026 from official town / state (DCR) / Trustees / Mass Audubon / Historic
New England / MBTA and event-organizer sources, cross-checked across multiple sources.
Every entry links its source; closures and unconfirmed dates are flagged. **Verify hours,
dates, and fees before you go** — they change seasonally.
