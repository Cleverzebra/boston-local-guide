# Boston Area Stuff to Do - custom edition

A personalized Greater Boston field guide built for one household: a vegetarian
family with a pickleball habit, a taste for flea markets and hidden rooftops, a
dog who expects weekend plans, and an ongoing hunt for good date ideas that
don't cost much. Free-to-moderate prices, locals' picks over tourist traps,
organized by season and weekday-vs-weekend.

Open **`index.html`**: a single, self-contained interactive page (HTML plus
inline styles, no build step, no dependencies). It is light/dark aware and hosts
on GitHub Pages at https://cleverzebra.github.io/boston-local-guide/. See
**`STATUS.md`** for how this repo stays the one canonical copy and how it gets
refreshed.

## How it works

One page, seventeen tabs across the top, with filters (price, dog-friendly,
date-worthy) that work across every guide. Pick a tab or filter and the cards
below update in place. "Decide for Me" and "This Week" read the device's date,
so they are current on every open.

| Tab | Covers |
|------|--------|
| Home | Who it's for, ready-made playbooks, year-at-a-glance calendar |
| Decide for Me | Answer three or four questions (day, inside or outside, time, budget) and it picks one in-season plan, with live Boston weather |
| This Week | Dated events for the next nine days, soonest first, plus the every-week backbone by day |
| Outdoors | Outdoor recreation plus free pickleball courts, trails, rail-trails, paddling, swimming ponds, hidden rooftop gardens and free skyline views |
| Beaches | Beaches with parking, stickers, transit, amenities, and when dogs are allowed on the sand |
| Day Trips | Day trips across seven regions, each town with a verified vegetarian lunch |
| Eats | Vegetarian-first restaurant guide: fully veg rooms, veg-royalty omnivore spots, brunch, Thai in depth, a by-cuisine table where the carrot flags the vegetarian order, date tables, bakeries, ice cream, a $12 hall of fame, recent closures, and where to buy produce |
| Date Ideas | Free, under $25, and under $60 dates, all age-rule checked, plus the frozen-yogurt and sweets circuit and the ten best dates scripted |
| Dog Days | Off-leash spots, dog beaches by season, dog-welcome patios, day trips, MBTA pet rules, emergency vets |
| Events | Fairs, farmers markets, festivals, music festivals, flea and craft markets, thrifting |
| Indoors | Rainy days: quirky and free museums, candlepin and barcades, indie cinemas, first-run multiplexes, Level 99, cheap live music, libraries and bookstores |
| Crafting | Craft-supply stores, one-night workshops, studios and real classes, learning to sew, bead and jewelry supplies |
| Shops | Independent, locally owned stores: boutiques, gifts and makers, curated vintage, thrift |
| Work Spots | Cafes, libraries, and day desks for working away from home |
| By Neighborhood | The short list for Charlestown, Somerville, Everett and Malden, Lynn and Nahant, Dedham and Needham, Lexington and Arlington |
| Christmas | The three anchors (Nutcracker, Revels, Holiday Pops), strolls, lights and rinks, gingerbread teas and hotel bars, Christmas day trips, and what to do when guests are in town |
| Calendar | Day-by-day, month by month, July 2026 to June 2027; the home month cards click straight through |

## Notes on the research

- Compiled July 2026 from official town, state, DCR, Trustees, MBTA, and
  organizer sources, cross-checked across multiple sources, including age
  policies for every venue in Date Ideas and dog policies for every spot in Dog
  Days.
- Refreshed weekly by an automated pass (dated events, confirmations, new
  festivals). Full audit with link check and fact re-verification: Fri Sept 4,
  2026.
- Verify before you go. Fees, hours, and dates change seasonally; every entry
  links its official source, and closures are flagged inline.
