# Boston Area Stuff to Do — custom edition

A personalized Greater Boston field guide built for one household: a vegetarian
family with a pickleball habit, a taste for flea markets and hidden rooftops, a
dog who expects weekend plans, and an ongoing hunt for good date ideas that
don't cost much. Free-to-moderate prices, hidden gems over tourist traps,
organized by season and weekday-vs-weekend.

Open **`index.html`** — it's a single, self-contained interactive page (HTML +
inline styles, no build step, no dependencies). It's light/dark aware and hosts
fine on GitHub Pages. See **`STATUS.md`** for how this repo stays the one
canonical copy.

## How it works

One page, eleven tabs across the top, with filters (price, dog-friendly,
date-worthy) that work across every guide. Pick a tab or filter and the cards
below update in place.

| Tab | Covers |
|------|--------|
| **Home** | Who it's for, ready-made playbooks, year-at-a-glance calendar |
| **Outdoors** | Outdoor recreation + **free pickleball courts**, trails, paddling, swimming ponds, hidden rooftop gardens & skyline views |
| **Beaches** | Beaches with parking, stickers, transit, amenities — plus when dogs are allowed on the sand |
| **Day Trips** | Day trips across seven regions, each town with a **verified vegetarian lunch** |
| **Eats** | Vegetarian-first restaurant guide — fully veg/vegan rooms, veg-royalty omnivore spots, a **brunch** roundup, and a **by-cuisine** table (Italian, pizza, Thai, sushi, Indian/Nepali, Afghan, Ethiopian, West African & more) where 🥕 flags the vegetarian order; plus date tables and a $12 hall of fame |
| **Date Ideas** | Affordable date ideas: free / under-$25 / under-$60, all all-ages-verified with age-rule fine print, plus a frozen-yogurt & sweets circuit |
| **Dog Days** | Off-leash spots, dog beaches by season, dog-welcome patios, day trips, MBTA pet rules, emergency vets |
| **Events** | Fairs, farmers markets, festivals, flea & craft markets, thrifting |
| **Indoors** | Rainy-day: quirky/free museums, candlepin & barcades, indie cinemas, first-run multiplexes, Level 99, cheap live music |
| **Crafting** | Craft-supply stores, one-night workshops, and studios |
| **Calendar** | Month-by-month, July 2026 – June 2027: weekly recurring markets & free nights, plus dated fairs, feasts and festivals — home month cards click straight through |

## Notes on the research

- Compiled and refreshed **July 2026** from official town/state/DCR/Trustees/MBTA
  and organizer sources, cross-checked across multiple sources — including age
  policies for every venue in Date Ideas and dog policies for every spot in Dog
  Days.
- **Verify before you go.** Fees, hours, and dates change seasonally; every
  entry links its official source, and closures are flagged inline (this edition
  caught several 2025–26 closures other guides still list).
